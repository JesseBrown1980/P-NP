## Affine obstruction

For a CNF of non-tautological clauses on distinct variables, preserve each clause as one factor and substitute x=A y+b over F_2, with A invertible. Every column in the union of the relevant rows of A is essential to the substituted factor: choose x falsifying the clause, invert to y, then flipping that column changes at least one of those original literals to true. A nonzero determinant guarantees a perfect matching between rows and columns of nonzero entries. The matched coordinate of every original variable is therefore present in each corresponding transformed clause scope. All original primal edges survive under that matching; treewidth cannot decrease. This statement excludes elimination, factor merging, auxiliary variables, nonlinear transformations, and shadow projection. Local 24 cases (14 SAT, 10 UNSAT, randomized CNF, pigeonhole and Tseitin), six invertible transformations each: 144 checks, no greedy-width improvement, 62,592 exhaustive address comparisons for selected small cases.

## Shadow-address theorem

For an internal set Z and boundary B, take the product of all factors incident to Z; define the count shadow W(b)=sum_z product f_i(b,z). Other factors depend only on outside coordinates; substituting W preserves the full model count exactly. For positive W store a choice of internal address with positive weight. Reverse committed projections to reconstruct a witness, then check source CNF. For each bounded group whose incident union has at most six variables, all rows can be computed and checked at cost bounded by 64 times the number of incident factors. This is collective factor elimination, not affine relabeling. If W is the indicator of an affine equation or identically one, it admits an exact GF(2) or constant representation; otherwise leave it uncommitted in this gate. Exact count requires multiplicities, not just support. A single witness suffices to recover one satisfying assignment, not the complete set of assignments. For the committed unit-valued tables in this audit there is exactly one extension per feasible boundary; witness is therefore sufficient to reconstruct all feasible local rows. This does not establish compact projections for arbitrary SAT.

The existing sum-and-signed-difference triple witness reconstructs three count entries using (s+d1)/3, (s+d2)/3, (s-d1-d2)/3. Local audit used this arithmetic on boundary rows, with zero padding; independent reverse enumeration verifies the entries and choices. Same-sum changes require independent source comparison, not just internal divisibility. All three pairwise projections of even and odd 3-bit parity are identical; the full relations are disjoint. A lossy shadow or agreement of marginals alone is insufficient.

## Executed results

Twenty randomized-label compact-gate-encoded prism Tseitin fixtures (k=3,4,5,8,12; two charges and two shuffles); 27 through 108 total variables. Parity hidden behind ordinary AND/OR clauses and equality. Greedy one-variable projection stalled in all 20 at cap 5. The connected-group search of internal sets of size at most three, incident union at most six, returned 20/20 exact counts against independent graph rank formula, with 10 satisfying source witnesses and 10 affine contradictions. Four smallest counts also matched original-CNF DPLL. Eight controls removing one defining clause all HOLD, with independent reference counts positive; no false UNSAT. Trial cost across 20 intact cases: 14,822 candidate sets, 20,160 evaluated trial rows, 172,352 factor reads, 132,064 classification rows, 14,656 accepted rows, 256 committed projections, 714 eliminated coordinates (aggregate), 256 derived affine relations. Each accepted union width <=6. This is a standard bounded variable elimination plus affine recognition method on a structured family; no comparison to optimized SAT preprocessors yet.

Second audit independently re-enumerated every committed projection (including partial progress on damaged HOLD cases): 304 projections, 17,600 reverse-enumerated union rows, 2,432 boundary rows, 1,216 positive witness rows, 912 signed-triple roundtrips, 912 divisibility and 912 same-sum negative controls. Native test exercises the actual FrozenMatrix on a small fixed parity gate control, not the complete blind group-discovery solver. Native freeze retains full cells plus witnesses; no physical storage compression or full historical Path 2 integration claimed.
